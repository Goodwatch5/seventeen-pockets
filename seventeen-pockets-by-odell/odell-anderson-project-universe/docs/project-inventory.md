## Interpretation

These repositories and services represent coordinated operational forms of one platform:

* **Rightway** is the interface form
* **seventeen-pockets** is the orchestration form
* **solar-connect** is the data form
* **nextmyteam** is the collaboration form
* **deepagent** is the affiliated intelligence form
