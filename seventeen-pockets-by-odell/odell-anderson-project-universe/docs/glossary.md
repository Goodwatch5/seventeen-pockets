# Glossary

This glossary defines the recurring platform language used across the Seventeen Pockets / Rightway ecosystem.

The purpose of this page is to keep documentation, planning, and presentation language consistent.

***

## Platform

The **platform** is the unified system-level identity formed by the Seventeen Pockets / Rightway ecosystem. It is the whole, not just one repository.

## Ecosystem

The **ecosystem** is the multi-project structure through which the platform is expressed. It includes the controlled core repositories and, where relevant, integrated external services.

## Operational form

An **operational form** is a specialized functional expression of the platform. Different repositories or services may represent frontend, backend, data, collaboration, or AI forms of the same system.

## Ecosystem home

The **ecosystem home** is the primary repository used to document and coordinate the platform at the system level. In the current structure, Rightway serves this role.

## Core project

A **core project** is a controlled repository that is part of the primary platform story and active ecosystem structure.

## Integrated external

An **integrated external** is a connected service or repository that contributes to the platform architecture but is not controlled from the same repository set or namespace.

## Interface form

The **interface form** is the user-facing layer of the platform. It provides visibility, entry points, and direct interaction.

## Backend form

The **backend form** is the orchestration and logic layer of the platform. It handles APIs, system coordination, and service behavior.

## Data form

The **data form** is the metrics, collection, and system-intelligence layer of the platform.

## Collaboration form

The **collaboration form** is the team coordination and workflow-support layer of the platform.

## AI form

The **AI form** is the intelligence, reasoning, and agent-service layer of the platform.

## Orchestration

**Orchestration** is the coordination of system logic, service relationships, and cross-project behavior across the platform.

## Deployment truth

**Deployment truth** is the clearest current interpretation of where platform deployment authority comes from and how deployment paths should be described.

## Current state

The **current state** is the concise operational picture of where the platform stands now, including what is active, what is incomplete, and what is being clarified.

## Status model

The **status model** is the canonical vocabulary used to classify projects and services consistently across platform documentation.

***

## Platform principle

**One platform. Multiple operational forms. Unified by design.**
