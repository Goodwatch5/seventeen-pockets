# Seventeen Pockets — Brand Manifest

**Owner:** Odell Anderson **Part of the ODell Ecosystem | Branded by ODell | Powered by ODell and Light Creations**

***

## Core Concept

**Functional, modular pieces** — each product contains 'seventeen pockets' of utility and narrative.

Every Seventeen Pockets product is designed with intentionality, combining practical functionality with meaningful storytelling. The "seventeen pockets" metaphor represents the multiple layers of value, purpose, and craftsmanship embedded in each piece.

***

## Brand Pillars

1. **Utility-First Design**
   * Form follows function
   * Every element serves a purpose
   * Thoughtful, intentional design
2. **Thoughtful Storytelling**
   * Each product has a narrative
   * Connection between maker and user
   * Meaningful communication
3. **Sustainable Materials**
   * Responsible sourcing
   * Environmental consciousness
   * Long-lasting quality
4. **Collaborative Craftsmanship**
   * Partnership with artisans
   * Community-driven creation
   * Shared values and vision
