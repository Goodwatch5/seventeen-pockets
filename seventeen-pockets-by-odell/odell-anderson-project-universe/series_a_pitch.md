## Slide 3: The Solution

### Rightway: Edge-First Clinical Intelligence

**Three Deployment Models:**

1. **Offline Mode** (Primary)
   * Works with zero internet
   * Local AI inference (Google MedGemma)
   * Local database (all data stays in clinic)
   * Perfect for rural/field medicine
2. **Hybrid Mode** (Recommended)
   * Local-first operation
   * Optional cloud sync (encrypted)
   * Best of both worlds
3. **Connected Mode** (Enhanced)
   * Cloud-assisted AI
   * Real-time analytics
   * Multi-clinic insights

**Key Innovation:**

> We're the only platform where the cloud is optional, not required.
