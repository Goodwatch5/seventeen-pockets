## Key Innovations

### Weight-Sparse Transformers

* Extreme sparsity: 1 in 1000 nonzero weights
* Enforced during training, not post-hoc pruning
* Maintains model capability while reducing parameters 1000x

### Interpretable Circuits

* Fine-grained node definitions (neurons, attention channels, residual channels)
* Task-specific circuit extraction with geometric mean edge counting
* 16x smaller circuits compared to dense model equivalents
* Explicit algorithms for quote detection, bracket counting, variable tracking

### Sparse-Dense Bridges

* Encoder-decoder pairs mapping sparse to dense activations
* Enables circuit interventions in production dense models
* Allows interpretability research on real-scale systems

### Circuit Visualization

* Interactive circuit graphs with node/edge inspection
* Task-specific circuit dashboards
* Decision path tracing for model predictions
